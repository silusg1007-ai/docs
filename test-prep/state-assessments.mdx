## Recommended Study Plan

Use this step-by-step plan to work through your state exam topics systematically. Following each step in order builds understanding before moving to practice — the same progression most state exams reward.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify your exam topics">
    Find your state's exam blueprint or tested standards list. Most state education department websites publish a content outline or standards document you can use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter for matching content">
    Use ScienceLab's subject and grade-level filters to find videos, labs, and worksheets that match each topic on your exam blueprint.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the concept video">
    Watch the concept video for each topic before moving to the lab. The video builds the background knowledge you'll need to make sense of your lab data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the virtual lab">
    Run the virtual lab for the topic. Collect your data carefully and work through all the steps — many state exams include questions about lab procedures and data interpretation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the worksheet or CER template">
    Download and complete the printable worksheet or CER template for the topic. Writing out your reasoning is one of the best ways to consolidate what you've learned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review vocabulary">
    Review any vocabulary lists for the topic. State exams frequently test scientific terminology, and knowing the precise definitions helps with both multiple-choice and constructed-response questions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Many state science exams include a laboratory component or lab-based questions — ScienceLab virtual labs give you hands-on practice with the same investigative skills tested on these exams. Don't skip the data collection steps, even if you're in a hurry.
</Tip>
