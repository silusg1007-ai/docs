ScienceLab lets you assign any combination of concept videos, virtual labs, and worksheets to your class. Assigned content appears directly in each student's dashboard, making it easy for them to find what they need without hunting through the library. You can assign to an entire class at once or target individual students.

## Creating an Assignment

You can assign content from any content page in the ScienceLab library — no need to navigate back to your dashboard first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse to the content">
    Find the video, virtual lab, or worksheet you want to assign using the library search or subject browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Assign">
    On the content page, click the **Assign** button. This opens the assignment configuration panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your audience">
    Choose the class you want to assign the content to. To assign to specific students only, toggle to **Individual Students** and select names from your roster.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a due date (optional)">
    Pick a due date from the calendar. Students will see the due date on their dashboard, and the platform will flag late submissions for you automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add instructions (optional)">
    Include notes or instructions for your students — for example, which sections to focus on or how you'd like them to record their data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the assignment">
    Click **Confirm Assignment**. The content will appear in each selected student's dashboard immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Assign the concept video 1–2 days before the lab for best results — students who watch the video first tend to complete labs faster and submit higher-quality written responses.
</Tip>

## Assigning a Sequence

For a complete lesson experience, ScienceLab lets you bundle related content into a single assignment sequence. A sequence walks students through your lesson in a structured order.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Watch" icon="play">
    Start with a concept video to build background knowledge before students touch the lab.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Lab" icon="flask">
    Students complete the virtual lab, collecting and analyzing data in a guided environment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reflect" icon="pen">
    Close with a worksheet or CER prompt to solidify learning and produce a written artifact.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

To create a sequence, click **Create Sequence** in the assignment panel. Add each piece of content in the order you want students to complete it. Students see the steps listed in their dashboard and must complete each one before moving to the next.

Sequences are especially useful for homework assignments, sub-day coverage, or flipped classroom lessons where students work at their own pace.

## Managing Assignments

All of your active assignments are visible in one place, so you can keep things organized as your class moves through units.

- Navigate to **My Assignments** in the teacher dashboard to see every current assignment and its class-level completion status.
- Click any assignment to **edit the due date**, update your instructions, or remove the assignment entirely.
- When a unit wraps up, click **Mark as Complete** to archive the assignment. Archived assignments remain visible in your history but no longer appear in students' active dashboards.

<Note>
  Removing an assignment does not delete student work. Any submissions already made are preserved in student activity logs and your grading history.
</Note>
