The ScienceLab class dashboard gives you a real-time view of every student's progress — lab completions, quiz scores, and time spent on task — all from a single screen. Whether you're teaching one class or several, everything you need to monitor and support your students is in one place.

## Dashboard Overview

When you open the dashboard, you'll see a summary of your class's activity at a glance. The following metrics are always visible at the top of your class view:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Labs Completed" icon="flask">
    The percentage of assigned labs your class has submitted, updated in real time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Average Quiz Score" icon="chart-bar">
    A rolling average of quiz scores across all graded assessments for the class.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Time on Platform" icon="clock">
    Total cumulative time your students have spent working in ScienceLab.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Below the summary, each student appears as an individual row in the roster. Each row shows that student's current lab status, which falls into one of four states:

- **Not Started** — the student hasn't opened the assignment yet
- **In Progress** — the student has opened the lab but hasn't submitted
- **Submitted** — the student has submitted and is awaiting your feedback
- **Graded** — you've reviewed the submission and added a score

You can filter the roster by class, date range, or specific assignment using the controls at the top of the table.

<Note>
  The dashboard updates in real time — you can monitor a live lab session as students work, watching their status change from "In Progress" to "Submitted" the moment they finish.
</Note>

## Setting Up Your Class

Before students can appear in your dashboard, you'll need to create a class and share your class code.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new class">
    Click **Create Class** from your dashboard home screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter class details">
    Provide your class name, grade level, and subject area. These details help ScienceLab surface relevant content recommendations for your students.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share your class code">
    Once your class is created, you'll see a unique class code. Share this code with your students — they enter it when signing up for ScienceLab or in their account settings under **Join a Class**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Students join your roster">
    As students enter your class code, they appear automatically in your roster. No manual approval is required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reading Student Reports

Digging into an individual student's work is just a few clicks away.

- Click any student's name in your roster to open their detailed activity log.
- Review timestamps showing exactly when they started a lab, if they paused, and when they submitted.
- Read their full responses to analysis questions and CER (Claim-Evidence-Reasoning) templates directly in the report view.

This level of detail makes it easy to understand not just whether a student finished, but how they engaged with the material — and where they may have struggled.

## Grading Submitted Labs

Once a student submits a lab, it moves into your grading queue automatically.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pending Review">
    Navigate to the **Pending Review** section in your dashboard. All submitted labs waiting for feedback are listed here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the submission">
    Click a submission to read the student's responses, data entries, and written analysis in full.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your score and feedback">
    Enter a numeric score and written comments directly in the dashboard. Your feedback can address specific responses or provide overall guidance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return the graded lab">
    Click **Submit Feedback**. The student will see your score and comments the next time they log into their own dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
