ScienceLab provides printable resources for every topic on the platform — worksheets, vocabulary lists, and Claim-Evidence-Reasoning (CER) templates — to help you prepare before a lesson, practice during one, and reflect on what you've learned afterward. Every resource is free, downloadable as a PDF, and formatted for standard letter-size paper.

## Types of Resources

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Worksheets" icon="file-lines">
    Topic-specific practice problems, diagram labeling activities, and reading guides that reinforce the concepts from each video or lab.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Vocabulary Lists" icon="book-open">
    Key terms with clear, student-friendly definitions for each unit. Great for studying before a quiz or keeping handy while working through a lab.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CER Templates" icon="pen-to-square">
    Structured templates for writing scientific arguments using the **Claim-Evidence-Reasoning** framework. Each template includes sentence starters and prompts to guide your thinking.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Post-Lab Analysis Questions" icon="magnifying-glass-chart">
    Printable versions of the analysis questions from virtual labs — useful if your teacher wants you to complete them on paper or if you'd like to review your answers after submitting.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to Access Resources

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the Content Page">
    Every video and virtual lab page has a **Resources** tab. Click it to see all downloadable materials linked to that specific topic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the Resources Library">
    Visit the **Resources** section of the main navigation to search or filter all available materials by subject, grade band, or resource type — without needing to navigate through a specific video or lab first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Your Assigned Section">
    If your teacher assigned a specific worksheet or template, it will appear in the **Assigned** section of your dashboard alongside any videos or labs they've assigned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  All resources are aligned to NGSS standards. Each PDF includes the relevant standard code in the footer so you and your teacher can easily connect the material to your curriculum.
</Info>

## Printing and Downloading

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download as a PDF">
    Click the **PDF icon** next to any resource to download it to your device. The file will save to your default downloads folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print from Your Browser">
    Open the downloaded PDF and press **Ctrl+P** (Windows) or **Cmd+P** (Mac) to open your browser's print dialog. Select your printer and click **Print**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Your Paper Size">
    All resources are formatted for standard **8.5" × 11"** paper. If your printout looks cut off, check that your printer's paper size setting matches and that scaling is set to **Fit to Page**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If your teacher accepts digital submissions, you don't need to print at all. Save your CER template or worksheet PDF to your device and type your responses directly into the PDF using a free tool like Adobe Acrobat Reader or your school's preferred platform.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Resources are updated periodically to reflect changes in NGSS standards and curriculum alignment. If you downloaded a resource more than a semester ago, it's worth re-downloading to make sure you have the latest version.
</Note>
