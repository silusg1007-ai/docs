ScienceLab's concept videos are short, focused lessons — typically 5–10 minutes each — tied directly to NGSS standards. Whether you watch before a virtual lab to build background knowledge or after to reinforce what you discovered, each video is designed to make key science concepts clear and approachable.

## Finding Videos

You can browse or search the full video library from the **Videos** section of the ScienceLab navigation.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Browse by Subject" icon="flask">
    Choose from biology, chemistry, physics, earth science, or ecology to see all videos in that discipline.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Browse by Grade Band" icon="graduation-cap">
    Filter by K–2, 3–5, 6–8, or 9–12 to find videos matched to your level.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Search by Topic" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Use the search bar at the top of the Videos page to find a specific concept — for example, "photosynthesis" or "Newton's laws."
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  If your teacher assigned a video, you don't need to search for it. Head to the **Assigned** section on your dashboard and it will be waiting for you there.
</Tip>

## Video Player Controls

The ScienceLab video player includes everything you need to learn at your own pace.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Playback Controls" icon="circle-play">
    Use the play/pause button and the rewind button to move through the video. Adjust the speed to **0.75x**, **1x**, **1.25x**, or **1.5x** — slowing down is great for tricky concepts, and speeding up helps on review.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Closed Captions" icon="closed-captioning">
    Toggle captions on or off using the **CC** button in the player toolbar. Captions are available for every video on the platform.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Full-Screen Mode" icon="expand">
    Click the full-screen icon in the bottom-right corner of the player to expand the video to your entire screen. Press **Esc** to exit.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Volume" icon="volume-high">
    Use the volume slider in the player toolbar to adjust audio. Headphones are recommended in shared spaces.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## After Watching

Finishing the video is just the beginning — use the follow-up resources to lock in what you learned.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Answer the Quiz Questions">
    Some videos end with a short knowledge check. Answer the questions directly in the player to test your understanding before moving on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download Supporting Resources">
    Click the **Resources** tab below the video to download the accompanying vocabulary list or worksheet as a PDF. These are great for studying or filling in during a second watch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Linked Virtual Lab">
    Many videos are paired with a hands-on virtual lab. Click **Open Lab** on the video page to jump straight into the experiment and apply what you just learned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your watch history is saved automatically. If you need to revisit a video for a quiz or lab report, you can find recently watched videos in the **History** section of your dashboard.
</Note>
