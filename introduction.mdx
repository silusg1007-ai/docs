Welcome to ScienceLab — a 100% free science education platform built for K-12 students, teachers, and parents. Our mission is to make high-quality science learning accessible to everyone, with no subscriptions, no credit cards, and no paywalls standing in the way. Whether you're a student exploring a virtual lab, a teacher building a lesson plan, or a parent supporting learning at home, ScienceLab gives you the tools you need to spark curiosity and deepen understanding.

## Built Around NGSS Standards

Every piece of content on ScienceLab is aligned to the **Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS)**. Resources are organized into four grade bands so you can always find content that matches where your students or child are in their learning journey:

| Grade Band | Grades |
|------------|--------|
| Early Elementary | K–2 |
| Upper Elementary | 3–5 |
| Middle School | 6–8 |
| High School | 9–12 |

Use the NGSS standards filter in the Content Library to browse by disciplinary core idea, science and engineering practice, or crosscutting concept — so every lesson connects to the bigger picture of science learning.
