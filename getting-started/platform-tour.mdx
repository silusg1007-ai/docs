ScienceLab is organized into a set of core areas, each designed to support a different part of the science learning experience. Whether you're watching a two-minute concept video, running an interactive simulation, or downloading a printable worksheet, everything is just a click or two away from your main dashboard. This tour walks you through each section so you can start finding and using content with confidence.

## Home Dashboard

When you log in, you land on your **Home Dashboard** — your personalized starting point on ScienceLab. The dashboard shows a feed of recently accessed content so you can pick up where you left off, along with recommended videos, labs, and resources based on your grade level or the subjects you teach. Notifications about new assignments (for students) or class activity (for teachers) also appear here.

<Info>
  Your Home Dashboard updates automatically as you use the platform. The more content you explore, the more relevant your recommendations become.
</Info>

## Content Library

The **Content Library** is where you browse and discover all of ScienceLab's learning materials. You can explore content by:

- **Subject** — Life Science, Physical Science, Earth & Space Science, Engineering & Technology
- **Grade Band** — K–2, 3–5, 6–8, or 9–12
- **NGSS Standards** — filter by Disciplinary Core Idea (DCI), Science and Engineering Practice (SEP), or Crosscutting Concept (CCC)
- **Content Type** — videos, virtual labs, worksheets, or CER templates

Use the search bar at the top of the library to find content by keyword, topic, or standard code (for example, `MS-PS1-1`).

## Virtual Labs

The **Virtual Labs** section hosts ScienceLab's browser-based science simulations. Labs cover a wide range of topics across physics, chemistry, biology, and earth science — and they run entirely in your browser with no downloads, plug-ins, or app installations required.

Each lab includes:

- A brief background reading section and guiding questions
- An interactive simulation with adjustable variables
- An embedded data table for recording observations
- A reflection prompt tied to NGSS science and engineering practices

<Tip>
  Teachers can assign specific virtual labs to their class directly from the lab page. Students will see assigned labs highlighted on their Home Dashboard.
</Tip>

## Videos

The **Videos** section contains short, focused concept videos — typically two to six minutes long — covering key science ideas at every grade band. Each video includes:

- **Pause and replay controls** so you can review key moments
- **Closed captions** for accessibility
- **Linked resources** such as vocabulary lists and follow-up worksheets
- A short **comprehension check** at the end to reinforce learning

Videos are also embedded throughout the Content Library alongside related labs and worksheets for a connected learning experience.

## Worksheets & Resources

The **Worksheets & Resources** section provides printable and downloadable PDFs you can use in the classroom or at home. Available resources include:

- **Concept worksheets** aligned to specific NGSS standards
- **CER (Claim–Evidence–Reasoning) templates** for written science explanations
- **Lab report templates** designed to pair with virtual labs
- **Vocabulary reference sheets** for each grade band

All files are available in standard PDF format and are free to download and print as many times as you need.

<Note>
  CER templates are especially useful for developing scientific writing skills. Look for the CER badge on any resource that includes a structured Claim–Evidence–Reasoning prompt.
</Note>

## My Progress & Class Dashboard

What you see in this section depends on your role:

**Students — My Progress:** Track every video you've watched, every lab you've completed, and every worksheet you've submitted. Your progress view shows completion percentages by subject and grade band, plus any badges you've earned along the way.

**Teachers — Class Dashboard:** Monitor your entire class from a single view. See which students have completed assignments, review comprehension check scores, send reminders to students who haven't started yet, and export progress reports for parent-teacher conferences or administrative records.

<Warning>
  Class Dashboard data is only visible to the teacher who created the class. Students can only see their own progress — never another student's data.
</Warning>
