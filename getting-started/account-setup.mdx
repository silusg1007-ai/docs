ScienceLab is completely free for everyone — students, teachers, and parents alike. Creating an account takes just a few minutes and requires no credit card or payment information of any kind. Signing up gives you a personalized experience tailored to your role, so you see the tools and content most relevant to you right from the start.

## Create Your Account

Follow these steps to get your ScienceLab account up and running.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to sciencelab.com and click Sign Up Free">
    Open your browser and navigate to [sciencelab.com](https://sciencelab.com). Click the **Sign Up Free** button in the top-right corner of the homepage. You can sign up directly with your email or use a Google account for faster registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your role: Student, Teacher, or Parent">
    Select the role that best describes you. Your role determines which features and views are enabled in your account — you can always update this later from your profile settings if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your name, email, and create a password">
    Fill in your first and last name, provide a valid email address, and create a secure password. Passwords must be at least eight characters long and include at least one number or special character.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from ScienceLab. Click the **Verify My Email** link inside the message. If you don't see the email within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder, then use the **Resend Verification Email** option on the confirmation screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    Finish setting up your account with a few extra details. Students select their current grade level so the platform can surface age-appropriate content. Teachers add their school name, subject area, and grade band. Parents can optionally link to a child's student account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Teachers** gain access to the full class dashboard, assignment creation tools, and student progress reports once their account is set up. **Students** automatically receive personal progress tracking, a learning history, and achievement badges as they complete content.
</Note>

<Tip>
  If your teacher has already created a class, you don't need to set up anything special — just sign up as a Student and enter the **class code** your teacher provided. You'll be added to their class automatically and will see any assigned content right away.
</Tip>

## Log In After Setup

Once your account is verified, logging in is simple.

1. Go to [sciencelab.com](https://sciencelab.com) and click **Log In** in the top-right corner.
2. Enter the email address and password you used during sign-up — or click **Continue with Google** if you registered that way.
3. You'll land on your personalized Home Dashboard, where your recommended content and recent activity are waiting.

<Info>
  If you ever forget your password, click **Forgot Password?** on the login screen. A reset link will be sent to your registered email address within a few minutes.
</Info>
