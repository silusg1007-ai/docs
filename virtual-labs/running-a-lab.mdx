Each ScienceLab virtual lab walks you through a complete scientific investigation. Follow these steps to get the most out of the experience — from background reading to submitting your final lab report.

## Running Your Lab

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Lab">
    Find the lab from your dashboard, the Lab Catalog, or a teacher assignment. Click **Start Lab** to begin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the Background">
    The Background tab explains the scientific concepts you'll investigate. Read it carefully before moving on — the background context will help you form a stronger hypothesis and understand your results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Your Hypothesis">
    State your prediction about what will happen. Use the **"If...then...because"** format if provided. Your hypothesis is saved here and referenced in your final report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the Procedure">
    The Procedure tab gives you step-by-step instructions. Follow each step in order. You'll interact with the simulation directly on this tab — click, adjust, and observe as you would in a real lab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record Your Data">
    Enter all measurements and observations in the Data table as you go. Don't skip any rows — missing data will affect your analysis and your final score.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the Analysis Questions">
    The Analysis tab has questions about your data. Use your data table to answer them — don't guess. Reference specific values and units from your recorded measurements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Your Conclusion">
    Use the **CER template** (Claim, Evidence, Reasoning) to explain what your results mean. Reference specific data points from your table to support your claim.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Your Lab">
    Click **Submit** on the Conclusion tab. Your teacher will receive your completed report. You'll see a confirmation screen when your submission goes through successfully.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Saving and Returning

You don't need to finish a lab in one session. ScienceLab saves your progress continuously so you can return whenever you're ready.

- Your work is **automatically saved** every time you move between tabs
- To return to an in-progress lab, go to **My Labs** on your dashboard and click the lab name
- **Do not clear your browser cache** while a lab is in progress — this can erase unsaved simulation state

<Warning>
  Once you click **Submit**, your lab is locked. Make sure you've reviewed all your answers before submitting — you won't be able to make changes after the report is sent to your teacher.
</Warning>

## If Something Goes Wrong

Most issues during a virtual lab can be resolved quickly without losing your work.

- **Reload the page** if the simulation freezes — your data is saved automatically, so you won't lose your entries
- **If you lose your data**, contact your teacher — they can reset the lab for you so you can start fresh
- **For persistent issues**, use the **Report a Problem** link found on any lab page to send a bug report to the ScienceLab support team
