## Featured Lab — Acid/Base Titration

**Grades 9–12 | ~40 minutes**

The Acid/Base Titration lab replicates the precision and technique of a real titration — without the burets, glassware, or hazardous reagents.

- Add a titrant drop-by-drop to a solution containing an indicator and observe the color change at the equivalence point
- Record volume data for each trial and use stoichiometry to calculate the concentration of the unknown solution
- Ideal for **AP Chemistry** and **NY Regents Chemistry** review — the lab mirrors the procedure and data analysis expected on both exams

<Info>
  Chemistry labs in grades 9–12 generate quantitative data that you can use directly for stoichiometry calculations — bring a calculator!
</Info>

## Featured Lab — Chemical Reactions Observer

**Grades 6–8 | ~25 minutes**

The Chemical Reactions Observer introduces the four key types of evidence that a chemical change has occurred through a series of guided virtual experiments.

- Observe precipitation, gas production, color change, and temperature change by mixing virtual reagents in different combinations
- Record your observations in a structured data table for each reaction
- Analysis questions focus on distinguishing chemical changes from physical changes and identifying evidence types from your observations
