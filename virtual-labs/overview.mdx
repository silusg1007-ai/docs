ScienceLab's virtual labs are browser-based simulations that let you perform real science experiments — titrations, dissections, circuit building, genetics modeling — without any physical equipment, chemicals, or lab space. Everything runs in your web browser.

## What Makes Virtual Labs Effective

ScienceLab virtual labs aren't just animations — they're structured investigations designed around the same practices professional scientists use. Here's what sets them apart:

- **Structured like real lab investigations**: every lab follows the same progression — Background → Hypothesis → Procedure → Data Collection → Analysis → Conclusion — so you build scientific thinking habits with every experiment
- **Real-time simulated measurements**: the simulation responds to your actions just like real equipment would, including titration endpoint detection, live voltmeter readings, and projectile trajectory data
- **Built-in data tables and graphing tools**: record measurements directly in the lab and generate graphs from your data without switching to another application
- **CER (Claim-Evidence-Reasoning) conclusion templates**: a guided framework helps you write a complete, evidence-based conclusion every time
- **Teacher-visible submissions with scoring**: when you submit, your teacher receives your full lab report and can provide feedback or a grade directly in the platform

## Technical Requirements

ScienceLab virtual labs are designed to run on virtually any device you already have — no special hardware or software needed.

- Any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- No plugins, downloads, or software installation required
- Works on desktops, laptops, Chromebooks, and tablets
- Minimum recommended screen size: 10 inches for the best experience

## Lab Structure

Every virtual lab is organized into a series of tabs that guide you from start to finish. You move through them in order, and your work is saved as you go.

- **Tabs within each lab**: Background | Hypothesis | Procedure | Data | Analysis | Conclusion
- **Progress is saved automatically** each time you advance to a new tab or make entries in a data table
- **You can leave and return** to an in-progress lab at any time — just open "My Labs" from your dashboard to pick up where you left off

<Note>
  Some labs have timed sections that mirror exam conditions. Check the lab description before starting if you need uninterrupted time.
</Note>
