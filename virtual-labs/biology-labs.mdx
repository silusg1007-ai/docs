## Featured Lab — Punnett Square Simulator

**Grades 6–8 | ~30 minutes**

The Punnett Square Simulator lets you explore how dominant and recessive traits are passed from parents to offspring through hands-on genetic crosses.

- Select parent genotypes for one or two traits and run multiple crosses to generate offspring
- Observe phenotype ratios across a sample population and compare your results to Mendel's predicted outcomes
- Includes analysis questions focused on probability, heredity vocabulary, and interpreting Punnett square results

<Tip>
  Run the Punnett Square Simulator after watching the Genetics concept video for the best learning sequence — the video introduces dominant and recessive alleles so you'll know exactly what you're modeling in the simulation.
</Tip>

## Featured Lab — Virtual Frog Dissection

**Grades 9–12 | ~45 minutes**

The Virtual Frog Dissection lets you identify and examine major organ systems in detail — without a physical specimen, preservation chemicals, or dissection tools.

- Click to pin and label organs across the digestive, circulatory, and reproductive systems
- Complete an organ-function matching activity that tests your understanding of each structure's role
- Includes detailed diagrams and a downloadable anatomy reference sheet you can keep for review
