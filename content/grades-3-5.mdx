ScienceLab's grades 3-5 content builds scientific reasoning skills through inquiry-based videos, virtual investigations, and structured worksheets aligned to NGSS grades 3-5 performance expectations. At this stage, students move beyond observation and begin collecting data, identifying patterns, and constructing evidence-based explanations.

## Topics Covered

The 3-5 library spans all three NGSS disciplinary core idea areas with content that progressively deepens students' conceptual understanding.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Life Science" icon="leaf">
    Topics include **life cycles, ecosystems, inheritance and variation of traits**, and food webs — building from organisms to the systems they inhabit.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Earth Science" icon="globe">
    Students investigate **Earth's systems, weather and climate**, and the role of natural resources — including how humans interact with Earth's materials.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Physical Science" icon="atom">
    Content covers **forces and motion, energy**, and the properties and changes of matter, connecting observable phenomena to underlying scientific principles.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Content Types Available

Each topic in the 3-5 library includes a full set of resources to support both initial learning and deeper application.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Concept Videos" icon="play">
    Videos run **5-8 minutes** and include comprehension check questions at the end so students (and parents or teachers) can confirm understanding before moving on.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Virtual Investigations" icon="flask">
    Students conduct **guided virtual investigations** complete with data collection tables — introducing the structure of scientific inquiry in an accessible, no-mess format.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Structured Worksheets" icon="file-lines">
    Worksheets include **diagram labeling, data tables**, and short-answer questions that ask students to interpret results and explain their thinking.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Vocabulary Lists" icon="book-open">
    Every topic includes a **vocabulary list with definitions** to help students master the academic language they'll encounter in videos, labs, and class discussions.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Grades 3-5 students can begin using the CER (Claim-Evidence-Reasoning) template to write scientific arguments after completing a virtual investigation. It's a great way to practice putting data into words — a skill that carries through middle and high school science.
</Tip>
