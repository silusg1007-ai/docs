ScienceLab's earth science content helps students understand the planet they live on — from daily weather patterns and rock formation to plate tectonics and the solar system — with concept videos and interactive simulations at every grade level.

## Featured Virtual Simulations

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Plate Tectonics Simulation" icon="layer-group">
    **Grades 6–8**

    Animate convergent, divergent, and transform plate boundaries in real time — then observe how each type drives mountain building, subduction, and seafloor spreading.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rock Cycle Explorer" icon="circle-nodes">
    **Grades 6–8**

    Trace a rock sample through igneous, sedimentary, and metamorphic transformations, making choices at each stage that determine the next rock type.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Weather Pattern Simulator" icon="cloud-bolt">
    **Grades 3–5**

    Adjust temperature and moisture levels on an interactive map and watch how weather fronts form, move, and bring precipitation.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Earth science content pairs well with current events — run the Climate Change simulation after reading a news article about climate to connect real-world data to your lab experience.
</Tip>
