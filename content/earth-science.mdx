## Content Highlights by Grade Band

Earth science content at every grade band is built around concept videos, interactive simulations, and printable worksheets that address core NGSS performance expectations.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Weather and Seasons" icon="cloud-bolt">
    **Grades K–5**

    Concept videos and printable activities explore daily weather, seasonal changes, and how Earth's materials interact with water and wind.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Earth's Systems" icon="layer-group">
    **Grades 3–5**

    Content covers the geosphere, hydrosphere, and atmosphere — with diagram worksheets and vocabulary resources to support understanding of Earth's interconnected systems.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Plate Tectonics and Rock Cycle" icon="mountain">
    **Grades 6–8**

    Videos and interactive simulations cover how tectonic plates move, what drives the rock cycle, and how each process shapes Earth's surface over time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Climate Change and Earth History" icon="globe">
    **Grades 9–12**

    Advanced concept videos and data-analysis activities explore geologic time, climate feedback loops, and the evidence scientists use to reconstruct Earth's past.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Earth science content pairs well with current events — run the Climate Change simulation after reading a news article about climate to connect real-world data to your lab experience.
</Tip>
