ScienceLab's K-2 content introduces young learners to core science ideas through age-appropriate videos, interactive simulations, and printable activities — all aligned to NGSS K-2 performance expectations. Every resource is designed so that early readers can navigate independently, with icon-based interfaces and narrated audio throughout.

## What K-2 Students Learn

The K-2 library covers all three NGSS disciplinary core idea areas at a level that's accessible and engaging for young children.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Life Science" icon="seedling">
    Students explore **plants and animals**, what living things need to survive, and how organisms interact with their environments.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Earth Science" icon="cloud-sun">
    Content covers **weather patterns and seasons**, as well as Earth materials including soil, rocks, and water.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Physical Science" icon="hand-point-up">
    Young learners investigate **pushes and pulls**, the behavior of light and sound, and simple observable properties of matter.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Content Types Available

Every K-2 topic includes multiple resource types designed to work together as a complete learning experience.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Concept Videos" icon="play">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Interactive Simulations" icon="computer-mouse">

## Recommended Learning Path

Following a consistent sequence helps K-2 learners build understanding before jumping into open-ended exploration.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Watch the concept video">
    Start with the short video to introduce the topic. The narration and animations give students the vocabulary and background they need before touching the simulation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try the simulation">
    Open the interactive simulation and let your child explore. Icon-based navigation means they can interact confidently without relying on reading skills.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the printable activity">
    Print the accompanying worksheet to reinforce what your child just learned. Coloring and labeling activities are especially effective for cementing new concepts at this age.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  K-2 simulations are designed for touch screens — they work great on tablets! If your child uses a tablet at home or in the classroom, that's the ideal device for this grade band.
</Tip>
