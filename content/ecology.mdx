ScienceLab's ecology content explores how living things interact with each other and their environment — from simple food chains for young learners to complex ecosystem dynamics and human environmental impact for middle and high school students.

## Content Highlights by Grade Band

Ecology content at every grade band pairs concept videos with printable worksheets and activities so students can engage with ecological ideas at the right depth for their level.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Habitats and Food Chains" icon="tree">
    **Grades K–2**

    Short narrated videos introduce basic habitats, animal needs, and simple food chains — with coloring and labeling worksheets to reinforce the concepts offline.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Food Webs and Ecosystems" icon="diagram-project">
    **Grades 3–5**

    Videos and structured worksheets explore food webs, energy flow, biomes, and how human activity affects ecosystems — building toward NGSS 3-5 performance expectations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ecosystem Interactions" icon="chart-line">
    **Grades 6–8**

    Concept videos cover predator-prey relationships, competition, symbiosis, and biodiversity — with CER templates for students to write evidence-based arguments.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Population Dynamics and Conservation" icon="earth-americas">
    **Grades 9–12**

    Advanced content explores trophic energy flow, ecosystem services, and conservation biology — with data-analysis activities aligned to AP Environmental Science topics.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Ecology content pairs well with biology units on evolution and genetics — use the food web and ecosystem videos alongside Punnett square labs to connect natural selection to population-level outcomes.
</Tip>
