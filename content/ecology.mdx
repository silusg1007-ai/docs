ScienceLab's ecology content explores how living things interact with each other and their environment — from simple food chains for young learners to complex ecosystem dynamics and human environmental impact for middle and high school students.

## Featured Virtual Simulations

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Ecosystem Interactions Simulator" icon="chart-line">
    **Grades 6–8**

    Adjust predator and prey population sizes, then watch population curves oscillate over time as the ecosystem responds — and discover what keeps it stable.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Food Web Builder" icon="diagram-project">
    **Grades 3–5**

    Drag and drop organisms onto a canvas to construct food webs, then trace how energy moves from producers through multiple consumer levels.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Biodiversity Impact Simulation" icon="circle-minus">
    **Grades 9–12**

    Model how removing a keystone species triggers a cascade of population changes across an entire ecosystem, and explore what it takes to restore balance.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  The Ecosystem Interactions Simulator is particularly useful for understanding why real ecosystems need biodiversity — try removing the top predator and observe the cascade effect.
</Tip>
