ScienceLab's grades 6-8 content takes students deeper into scientific practice — running virtual labs with real data collection, analyzing evidence, and crafting written arguments — all aligned to NGSS middle school standards.

## Topics Covered

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Life Science" icon="dna">
    Cells, genetics and heredity (Punnett squares), ecosystems and human impacts, and natural selection.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Earth Science" icon="earth-americas">
    Plate tectonics, the rock cycle, weather systems, climate, and space and the solar system.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Physical Science" icon="atom">
    Forces and Newton's laws, energy transformations, waves and the electromagnetic spectrum, atoms and the periodic table, and chemical reactions.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Content Types

Every middle school topic includes a full set of learning resources designed to build both conceptual understanding and hands-on scientific skills.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Concept Videos" icon="circle-play">
    7–12 minute videos with embedded comprehension checks to keep you engaged and test understanding as you watch.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Virtual Labs" icon="flask">
    Full virtual labs with data tables, graphing tools, and analysis questions — including Punnett square simulations, chemical reaction experiments, and projectile motion modeling.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CER Templates" icon="file-pen">
    Claim-Evidence-Reasoning templates that guide you through writing a complete scientific argument based on your lab results.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Worksheets" icon="print">
    Structured lab report worksheets and printable resources you can complete independently or alongside class assignments.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Highlighted Virtual Labs

These labs are among the most widely used in the grades 6-8 library and target core NGSS middle school performance expectations.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Punnett Square Simulation" icon="table-cells">
    Explore dominant and recessive traits by running genetic crosses and predicting offspring ratios.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Projectile Motion" icon="arrow-trend-up">
    Model how launch angle and velocity affect the range of a projectile using interactive graphing tools.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chemical Reactions" icon="vial">
    Observe reactions between acids and bases, collect data on observable changes, and analyze your results.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Middle school labs include a full CER section — practice writing your reasoning in complete sentences for best results. The more detail you put into the Reasoning step, the better prepared you'll be for high school science writing.
</Tip>
