ScienceLab's grades 9-12 content is designed for high school students tackling biology, chemistry, and physics — including content that aligns with AP course frameworks and state assessment standards like the NY Regents.

## Advanced Virtual Labs

These labs are built for the depth and rigor of high school coursework — each one includes quantitative data collection, graphing, and structured analysis questions.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Acid/Base Titration" icon="droplet">
    Perform a complete titration with indicator color changes and endpoint calculation — mirroring the technique tested on chemistry exams.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Virtual Frog Dissection" icon="frog">
    Explore vertebrate anatomy in detail without a physical specimen, with labeled structures and guided observation questions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Electrical Circuit Building" icon="bolt">
    Design and test series and parallel circuits using a virtual voltmeter and ammeter to measure voltage, current, and resistance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Projectile Motion Simulator" icon="arrow-trend-up">
    Model how launch angle and initial velocity affect range and maximum height using interactive trajectory graphs — ideal for AP Physics kinematics problems.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
