ScienceLab's chemistry content introduces chemical concepts progressively — from simple observations about matter in elementary grades to quantitative reactions and electrochemistry in high school — supported by virtual labs that replace expensive and hazardous physical experiments.

## Safety Note

<Info>
  ScienceLab virtual chemistry labs let you explore reactions that would be too hazardous or expensive to perform in a school lab — no chemicals, flames, or protective equipment required.
</Info>

Every virtual lab includes embedded safety notes that mirror real-world lab protocol, so students build good habits even in a simulated environment.

<Tip>
  The Acid/Base Titration lab generates a full data set you can analyze to practice stoichiometry calculations — ideal for AP Chemistry prep.
</Tip>
