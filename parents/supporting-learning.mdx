ScienceLab gives parents a free, school-aligned way to support science learning at home — whether you're supplementing classroom work, homeschooling, or helping your child prepare for an exam. Everything on the platform is organized by grade band and subject, so you can quickly find content that matches exactly what your child is studying right now.

## Getting Started as a Parent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your free account">
    Go to [sciencelab.com](https://sciencelab.com) and click **Sign Up Free**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your role">
    Choose **Parent** when prompted to select your account type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your child's profile">
    Enter your child's grade level and the subjects they're currently studying so ScienceLab can surface the most relevant content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the Content Library">
    Browse the Content Library filtered to your child's grade band to see all available videos, virtual labs, and printable resources.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choosing the Right Content

ScienceLab organizes everything by grade band and subject, making it straightforward to find material that matches your child's current coursework.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Browse by Grade Band" icon="layer-group">
    Use the **K-2, 3-5, 6-8**, and **9-12** filters to stay within age-appropriate content. Each band reflects the NGSS performance expectations for those grade levels.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Browse by Subject" icon="flask">
    Filter by subject — biology, chemistry, physics, earth science, or ecology — to zero in on the exact topic your child is working on in school.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Start with Video" icon="play">
    Always open a **concept video** before launching a virtual lab. The video builds the background knowledge your child needs to get the most out of the hands-on simulation.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Pair each virtual lab with the accompanying vocabulary list to help your child learn the key terms before starting. Knowing the language makes the lab experience much smoother.
</Tip>

## Homeschooling with ScienceLab

ScienceLab is a strong fit for homeschool families because its content is comprehensive, standards-aligned, and completely free.

- **Standards alignment:** All content maps to the Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS), which form the basis of most state science standards across the U.S. You can be confident the material meets widely accepted benchmarks.
- **Written assessments:** Use the printable worksheets and CER (Claim-Evidence-Reasoning) templates as formal written assignments. They're ready to print and pair naturally with the virtual labs.
- **Full curriculum coverage:** ScienceLab covers biology, chemistry, physics, earth science, and ecology across every grade band — enough to support a complete K-12 science curriculum without needing a separate textbook.

<Note>
  If your child's teacher is already using ScienceLab, ask for the class code so their work gets tracked in the teacher's dashboard too. Joining a class gives the teacher visibility into your child's progress without changing anything about your parent account.
</Note>
