As a parent on ScienceLab, you can see a summary of everything your child has done on the platform — labs completed, videos watched, quiz results, and time on task. The progress view updates in real time, so you always have an accurate picture of where your child is and where they might need a little extra support.

## Parent Progress View

Your parent dashboard gives you a clear, at-a-glance overview of your child's activity without requiring you to sit next to them during every session.

## What You Can See

The progress view surfaces all of the following data for your child's account:

- **Labs completed** — with the date each lab was finished
- **Videos watched** — every concept video your child has viewed
- **Worksheets downloaded** — a record of printable resources saved or printed
- **Quiz scores** — both the average across all attempts and the score for each individual attempt
- **Time-on-task** — broken down by day and by week so you can spot patterns in study habits

<Info>
  Parent accounts do not share data with teachers unless your child joins a teacher's class using a class code. You stay in full control of what gets shared and with whom.
</Info>

## Encouraging Your Child

Progress data is most useful when you use it to have positive, specific conversations with your child about their science learning.

- **Celebrate completions.** Use the progress view to recognize finished labs and videos. Acknowledging specific accomplishments — "I saw you completed the ecosystems lab!" — is more motivating than generic praise.
- **Help them get unstuck.** If your child is struggling with a virtual lab, suggest they go back and watch the concept video for that topic first. Most labs have a paired video that covers exactly what they need to know.
- **No pressure to rush.** ScienceLab progress never expires. Your child can return to incomplete labs at any time, pick up right where they left off, and take as long as they need to feel confident before moving on.
